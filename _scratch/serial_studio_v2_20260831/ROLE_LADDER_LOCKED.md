# Locked role ladder

1. Countach Regular: surface identity and BENCH/MAIN role labels only.
2. Countach Bold Italic: the live-observability kicker and cross-device-delta label only.
3. Berkeley Mono: values, units, timestamps, provenance, health facts, and all diagnostic text.
4. Cyan identifies Bench; amber identifies Main. Green is reserved for exact confirmed states, not general decoration.
5. Instrument health precedes device values. Comparison precedes detail. Raw caveats remain visible at all times.

Countach does not carry dense telemetry, long prose, or small diagnostic rows. Berkeley Mono does not replace the restrained product identity layer.
