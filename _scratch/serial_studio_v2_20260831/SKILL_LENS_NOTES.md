# Skill lens notes

- Information architecture: every block answers instrument health, source health, device comparison, or evidence provenance.
- Typography: local fonts are treated as bounded roles rather than a theme applied everywhere.
- Accessibility: colour is redundant with text; unknown and unclassified states remain explicit; values retain units and ages.
- Security: the final surface has same-origin assets, a strict content-security policy, GET/HEAD-only serving, and no browser-to-Serial-Studio path.
- Evidence: the mock and final render were both inspected at 1440 x 900 and 1180 x 720. The final still is the primary comparison surface.
